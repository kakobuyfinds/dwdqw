How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet.

The most useful list is one you can verify. Move from broad inspiration to a specific item with clear measurements, a traceable seller and a realistic budget.

Start your product research ↗
01

Choose a category and purpose

Decide what you need: everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a summer T-shirt or a small accessory. Set a budget before comparing listings.

02

Inspect the original product details

Confirm the item, seller, color and size variant. A spreadsheet description may be incomplete or older than the live listing.

03

Compare measurements and QC references

Use actual measurements where provided. If QC photos are available, check that they correspond to the relevant item and variant.

04

Confirm ordering terms and total cost

Check current purchase eligibility, delivery options, fees and return terms directly with the service you intend to use.

A practical comparison framework

Better Kakobuy finds begin with better checks.

Budget beyond the listing

Estimate the delivered cost.

Item price + domestic delivery
+ service fees + international shipping
+ applicable taxes and conversion costs

Include only charges that apply to your order, and avoid counting fees twice if they are already bundled. International shipping may depend on destination, parcel dimensions, weight and delivery service.

Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges. Check the final checkout breakdown before paying.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list intended to help shoppers organize and compare item links. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, sizes and QC references. Entries are research leads, not guarantees of stock, quality or authenticity.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. Its browsing links open CAFANS, where you can inspect individual listings and available product details.

How should I check sizing before ordering?

Compare the listing's measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the units, measurement method and size variant. For shoes, compare the provided foot-length guidance or measurements rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Do QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help assess visible details, measurements and condition, but they do not establish authenticity. Check the seller's provenance, purchase documentation and applicable authentication or return options.

Is the listed product price the final delivered cost?

Usually not. Depending on the transaction, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes and currency conversion may add to the item price. Confirm the current checkout breakdown before paying.

Is this an official Kakobuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS-linked discovery guide. It is not presented as an official Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
